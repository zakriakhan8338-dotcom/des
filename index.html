ABOUT ZAKRIA KHAN

A Little About My Creative Journey

I'm Zakria Khan, also known as Zak Designer. I am a graphic designer who enjoys turning creative ideas into meaningful visual communication. My experience includes working on different business projects, developing brand visuals, creating social media content, and teaching graphic design.

I believe great design is not only about appearance; it is about communicating the right message. Whether it's crafting an authentic brand identity for an e-commerce platform or designing impactful promotional graphics for real estate and dining brands, I focus on precision and brand resonance.
